Recording your first sale

Open New sale from the sidebar or the Overview. Scan a barcode — the scanner types the code and presses Enter, which adds the line straight away.

If the customer pays less than the total, the panel asks for a customer and a due date, and the balance becomes a debt you can collect against later.

A mistake is never deleted. Void the sale with a reason and the stock returns, the debt cancels, and the receipt stays on the record.
